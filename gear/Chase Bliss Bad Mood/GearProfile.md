# Chase Bliss Bad Mood

**Summary** — Two-channel "ambient mischief" multi-effect (2026 Small Batch Bliss, $399, ships Nov 2026): an always-listening Micro-Looper (Burst / Radio / Mask) plus a Wet Channel of unorthodox spatial effects (Soup / Relay / Flip), both governed by a harmonized-stepping CLOCK sample-rate knob and bonded/obliterated by a global GLUE saturator. Not a MOOD MkII sequel — an inversion: "MOOD MKII captures the soft, dreamy side of ambience, and BAD MOOD explores the raw, reckless side."

## Why it's coming
Same "instant bliss" two-channel control scheme as the MOOD MkII already on the board, but built from scratch for the raw/reckless end — spectral resynthesis reverb (Soup), a no-decay repeater (Relay), time-spread harmonies (Flip), a loop step-sequencer (Burst), a five-station genre looper (Radio: Tape / Ambient / Orchestral / Shoegaze / Dance), and a threshold-triggered loop disguiser (Mask). The channels cross-modulate each other (CROSS / INPUT MOD) and everything exits through GLUE (warm gel → total thrash). MOOD-family workflow carries straight over: always-listening capture, freeze via hold, hidden options behind both footswitches, dip-switch customization, MIDIbox TRS MIDI on channel 2, 122 presets, Synth Mode.

## Signature uses
- **Getting-started texture:** grab a Radio loop, roll CLOCK back to slow/deepen everything in harmonized steps, then dunk it in Soup.
- **Synthetic Starter:** freeze Soup, record the frozen reverb into the micro-loop, unfreeze, play over the pad.
- **2-Track:** Relay's stable repeats + freeze as a second looper — layered compositions inside one pedal (use SYNC).
- **Chord Looper:** freeze Flip on one note, transpose with CLOCK, re-harmonize with MODIFY — the "strange little synth."
- **Dry Glue saturator:** DRY GLUE dip + MIX at zero = standalone stereo saturator / destroyer.

- 9V center-negative ~200 mA (lighter than MOOD MkII's ~270 mA). Buffered bypass (analog dry-thru); true bypass via 3× both-footswitch tap, but the always-listening looper stops in true bypass.
- MIDI channel 2 default; MIDI via CB MIDIBox (¼" TRS); the MIDI jack doubles as an external-footswitch input (TS, normally-open) that engages the Wet Channel.
