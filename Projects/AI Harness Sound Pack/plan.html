AI Harness Sound Pack · Project Plan

Tiobi Sound Plan

One bell voice, D major pentatonic, meaning carried by pitch shape and syllable count — designed from the research on how Slack, Apple, Google, and Microsoft do it, prototyped in code, and produced on the studio we already own. Listen to every prototype →

24 cues prototyped 5 families 3 half-day sessions to master $0 new gear

01The grammar

Every family says the same things in its own vocabulary. A listener who learns one family has learned them all:

Rising interval Incoming / needs your attention — ping, mention, question, connect, approve
Falling, resolving Completion / outgoing / closing — done, message-out, disconnect, deny
Low + damped Trouble. Never harsh, never loud — error is deliberately quieter than ping
More syllables, bigger milestone ding → da-DING → da-DING-a-LING; the sad ding droops

Three hard rules from the research: attention is earned by pitch shape, not loudness; everything under one second except the hero cue; every sound must survive being heard 100 times in a row.

04Production — three sessions on the existing rig

Google records Pixel sounds in the sound lead's home studio; Windows 11 is all synthesis. The studio this repo documents — Apollo x8, Logic, Komplete, Arturia, the pedalboard, iZotope — is already overqualified. Nothing needs to be bought.

1

Voice the family (half a day)

Audition three candidates for Tiobi's voice against the prototype MIDI: Kontakt kalimba (warmest match), Arturia DX7 V FM bell (the Windows 11 route), or OP-1 Field through the Deco (the most "us"). Pick one; bounce all 20 voice layers at 96 kHz/24-bit. Play the two-note cues by hand — micro-timing is a feature, not a flaw.

2

Strike layers + assembly (half a day)

Record tiny transients (SM57 or OP-1 mic, quiet closet, peaks ≈ −12 dBFS). Assemble each cue as voice + sine sub one octave down + strike — the industry-standard hybrid stack. Reamp done-big, connect, error through Deco/Chroma Console via the X-Amp for tape rounding. Render 5 randomized variants each for ping, message-in, progress — the anti-fatigue trick.

3

Mix, master, test, deliver (half a day)

  • Per cue: high-pass 150 Hz → gentle EQ (presence lift only on Tier 1) → transient shaping → Ozone true-peak limit at −1.5 dBTP
  • Loudness: −19 LUFS mono (Google's earcon spec) for Tier 2; Tier 1 up to ≈ −14; Tier 3 down to ≈ −24; final trims by ear, back to back
  • The gauntlet: 100× loop test · over a podcast and music · MacBook speaker, iPhone speaker, AirPods, monitors · mirrored pairs check
  • Deliver: mono WAV 48k/24 masters + lowercase .ogg + .m4a, same manifest.json schema as the prototypes

05Harness rules

  • Sound is never the only channel — every cue mirrors a visual state
  • Rate-limit like Android: max one notification sound per second; collapse bursts into one mention-weight cue
  • progress debounced to once per 2 s; fireworks on a cooldown (once a day is plenty)
  • Per-tier volume + a "minimal" preset keeping only mention, question, error
  • Honor OS mute and Do Not Disturb, always

06Legal lines

The method doesn't matter; recognizability does. Independent synthesis kills a sampling claim (17 U.S.C. §114(b) protects soundalikes of recordings) — but composition copyright and trademark don't care how a sound was made. A cue that makes people say "that's the Metal Gear sound" is litigable however it was created; a cue with "Metal Gear energy" is free.
What ships is 100% ours. Every shipping sound is synthesized or recorded in-house — full chain of title. Game and film inspiration is taken at the level of gesture and physics (contour, rhythm, structure), never recordings or recognizable melodies. Joke families carry internal: true and are excluded from release builds mechanically, not by promise. Third-party material, if ever used, is CC0 only.
